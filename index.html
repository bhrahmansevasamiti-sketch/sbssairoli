About the Samiti

Rooted in values. United in service.

Shree Brahman Seva Samiti, Airoli works to encourage community participation, preserve cultural and spiritual traditions, and coordinate activities that bring families and society members together.

What we do

Activities & community initiatives

The Samiti's activities create opportunities for devotion, cultural expression, volunteering and community connection.

🙏

Spiritual activities

Atharvashirsha recitation, Sankashti gatherings, bhajan and collective prayer.

🚩

Cultural programmes

Community celebrations, traditional observances and initiatives such as the Ekātmata Dindi.

🤝

Community service

Volunteer participation, member engagement and activities that strengthen social bonds.
